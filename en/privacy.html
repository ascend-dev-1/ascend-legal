This policy explains clearly what personal data is collected when you use ASCEND, why, how long it is kept, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights. This policy was originally written in French. This English version is provided for your convenience; in the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

In one sentence

ASCEND only collects the data strictly needed for your personalised coaching, stores it in France with Supabase, never sells it, never uses it for advertising, and lets you delete it at any time.

Article 02

Data collected

ASCEND only collects the personal data you provide voluntarily when using the Application, and the data strictly necessary for its technical operation.

Identity
Email address, first name, account creation date. Source: you (sign-up form, when creating your account).
Physical profile
Sex, age, height, current weight. Source: you (onboarding questions). Purpose: personalised calculation of your basal metabolic rate and calorie needs (Mifflin-St Jeor method).
Activity and goals
Declared training level, main goal (fat loss, recomposition, muscle gain, maintenance), weekly availability, estimated daily steps. Source: you.
Body measurements and tracking
Body measurements you choose to record (weight over time, waist, arm, thigh, chest, hips, calf). Source: you (manual entry).
Progress photos
Photos you freely choose to save to follow the evolution of your physique. Source: you. These photos remain strictly private, accessible only from your account, and are never published, shared or analysed without your explicit action.
Nutrition data
Meals you log (foods, quantities, estimated calories, macronutrients). Source: you.
Content sent to artificial intelligence features
Text descriptions of meals, meal photos sent for analysis, the summary of a finished workout sent for its debrief, and the useful context you have saved in ASCEND. This data is only sent when you voluntarily use the feature concerned. The generated answers may be saved in your history in order to provide and follow up the service.
Product searches
When you search for a packaged product or scan a barcode, the search terms or the barcode are sent to Open Food Facts, a public food product database, to retrieve the product's nutrition facts. No account information is sent with these requests.
Usage data
Active program, completed workouts, time of use, streaks, progression level reached in the Application. Source: generated automatically as you use the Application.
Minimal technical data
Unique account identifier (UUID), sign-in timestamp, operating system type (iOS or Android). Source: generated automatically by the servers when you sign in. No geolocation data, no advertising identifier, no IP address kept long term.
Payment data
No bank or card details are collected or stored by ASCEND. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on your platform. On behalf of ASCEND, RevenueCat processes a user identifier, the platform, the products purchased and the subscription status (active, expired or cancelled), without receiving your card details.
Data we do not collect

ASCEND collects no geolocation, contacts, microphone, calendar, photo library (other than the photos you explicitly add), browsing history or advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID) data, and no raw biometric data from third-party health sources (Apple Health, Health Connect, Google Fit) without your manual and explicit activation.

Article 03

Purposes and legal bases

In accordance with Article 6 GDPR, each processing operation relies on an identified legal basis.

Performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)

Most data is processed to perform the terms of use and the subscription you have entered into:

  • Creating and managing your user account
  • Calculating your calorie needs and your personalised training programs
  • Recording your progress (workouts, measurements, photos)
  • Syncing your data across your devices
  • Sending you functional notifications related to your program

Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

A few limited processing operations rely on the controller's legitimate interest:

  • Keeping the Application secure and preventing fraud or abuse
  • Improving the quality of the service using aggregated and anonymised usage data
  • Answering your support requests

You can object to this processing at any time (see Article 11).

Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

Some data may be kept to meet legal obligations, in particular accounting and tax obligations (10 years for accounting records relating to subscriptions).

Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)

Any optional feature going beyond what is strictly necessary (for example, behavioural reminder notifications, syncing with Apple Health or Health Connect, editorial email communications) is only activated with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time from the Application settings.

Article 06

Your data is not kept longer than necessary for the purposes described in Article 3.

You can request the permanent deletion of your account at any time. Deletion takes place within 30 days at the latest from receipt of your request.

Technical logs
Connection and technical logs are kept for a maximum of 12 months for security and fraud prevention purposes, then deleted.
Article 07

Data security

ASCEND implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of your data.

  • Encrypted communications: all communications between the Application and our servers are encrypted with TLS (HTTPS)
  • Encryption at rest: databases and file storage are encrypted on Supabase's servers
  • Strict isolation: each user can only access their own data, thanks to Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced on the server
  • Passwords: your password is never stored in plain text. It is hashed with a standard cryptographic algorithm (bcrypt) and cannot be recovered, not even by the publisher
  • Private photos: your progress photos are stored in private storage, only accessible through temporary signed URLs linked to your account
  • Stronger authentication: you can sign in with Apple or Google, delegating security to these recognised providers

Despite these measures, no system can guarantee absolute security. If an incident is likely to affect your data, you will be informed as soon as possible in accordance with Article 34 GDPR, and the incident will be notified to the CNIL under the conditions of Article 33 GDPR.

Article 08

Cookies and trackers

ASCEND is a native mobile application. It uses no browser cookies, no advertising pixels, no advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) and no third-party behavioural tracking tools for advertising purposes.

The Application only stores on your device the minimal technical data needed for it to work (sign-in session, display preferences, local cache). This data stays on your device and is never used for commercial purposes.

Article 09

Minors

The Application is strictly reserved for people aged 16 or over.

ASCEND does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a minor under 16 has created an account without your permission, contact us at ascend.webapp@gmail.com. The account will be suspended immediately and the associated data deleted.

Article 10

Profiling and automated decisions

ASCEND carries out no profiling for advertising or commercial purposes. Your data is never used to show you targeted advertising, nor shared with advertising networks or behavioural data brokers.

The Application automatically calculates your calorie needs, your macronutrients and your training program from the information you have provided. These calculations are strictly deterministic and based on published scientific formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor method for basal metabolic rate). They produce no legal effects concerning you and are not an "automated decision" within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

The artificial intelligence features generate answers from the information you choose to send. These answers may be inaccurate or incomplete and should be checked before you act on them. They are not a diagnosis, a prescription or medical advice, and produce no legal effect or binding decision concerning you.

You can always change your calorie and macronutrient targets manually in the Application, or ask for human assistance through the messaging features, depending on your subscription plan.

Article 11

Your rights

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 GDPR and the French "Informatique et Libertés" law, you have the following rights over your personal data at any time:

Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)

You can ask for confirmation that your data is being processed and obtain a copy of all the data concerning you.

Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)

You can ask for any inaccurate or incomplete data to be corrected. Most corrections can be made directly from your profile in the Application.

Right to erasure, or "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR)

You can request the permanent deletion of your data. Deletion takes place within 30 days at the latest, subject to legal retention obligations (in particular accounting obligations).

Deletion can be started in the Application from Profile → Settings → Delete my account, or requested without reinstalling the Application from the page Delete your ASCEND account.

Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)

You can ask for the processing of some of your data to be temporarily suspended, for example while a request for rectification is being checked.

Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You can ask to receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (JSON), in order to transfer it to another service.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You can object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest.

Right to withdraw your consent (Art. 7 GDPR)

For processing based on your consent (optional notifications, health syncing, etc.), you can withdraw your consent at any time from the Application settings, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Post-mortem instructions (Art. 85 of the French "Informatique et Libertés" law)

You can set instructions about what happens to your data after your death, either general (with a certified trusted digital third party) or specific to the Application (by writing to us directly).

Residents of other countries

If you live outside the European Union (for example in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada or Australia), you benefit from the same rights described above, in addition to any rights granted by the law of your country. ASCEND does not sell or share your personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at ascend.webapp@gmail.com, stating clearly which right you wish to exercise. We may need to verify your identity if there is any doubt about who is making the request. We answer every request within one month at most, which may be extended by two further months for particularly complex requests (with prior notice).

Exercising these rights is free of charge, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.
